QURANLENS EVIDENCE RULES v0.1

1. The Qur'an corpus is the only authoritative evidence source in Qur'an-only mode.
2. No web result, model memory, tafsir, hadith, scientific article, or user note may silently enter a Qur'an-only conclusion.
3. Every claim must cite its verse IDs.
4. Whole-Qur'an retrieval is required before synthesis.
5. Explicit wording outranks inference.
6. Exceptions, conditions, duration clauses, and limiting language must be checked before a conclusion.
7. Repeated or parallel passages must be compared together.
8. Linguistic certainty markers are evidence features, not automatic conclusions by themselves.
9. If the evidence is insufficient, output NOT ESTABLISHED.
10. Sensor readings may describe measurable physical phenomena only; they may not be used to claim detection of the unseen.
